# Every figure carries its source and its date

**No number ships without an inline source and the date it was published.**

## What is known, and how

- `[READ]` the convention and its failure modes — `patterns/provenance-and-dating/README.md`,
  2026-03-02.
- `[MEASURED]` unsourced figures were the single most common quality-gate blocker in Q2.

## Why it matters

An unsourced number cannot be checked, updated, or defended in a client meeting. It is an opinion
in the typography of a fact.

## What would change this

An internal working draft explicitly marked as such and never shown to a client.
