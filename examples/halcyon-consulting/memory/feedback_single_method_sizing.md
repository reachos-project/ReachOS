# One sizing method is one guess

**Never size a market by a single method; triangulate, or publish the range.**

## What is known, and how

- `[MEASURED]` top-down alone was out by roughly 3x on two engagements — post-mortem, 2026-06-02.
- `[INFERRED]` the error is not in the arithmetic but in the denominator — deduced from both cases
  sharing an over-broad market definition and nothing else.

## Why it matters

A point estimate from one method carries the same confident typography as a triangulated one, so
the reader cannot tell them apart. The range is the honest output.

## What would change this

A market with a single authoritative registry of participants, where the count is not an estimate.
