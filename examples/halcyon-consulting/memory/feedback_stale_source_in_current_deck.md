# A q2 figure carried into a q4 deck

**Never cite a source older than the quarter the deliverable describes.**

## What is known, and how

- `[REPORTED]` the client identified a Q2 market figure under a Q4 date — review call, 2026-07-18.
- `[MEASURED]` two further decks carried the same pattern — audit of `projects/` deliverables, 2026-07-19.

## Why it matters

A current date over a stale number does not read as an oversight; it reads as fabrication, and it
costs the whole deck its credibility rather than the one line.

## What would change this

A source that publishes only annually, where the quarter is genuinely the newest available — then
the age is stated in the deck instead of hidden.
