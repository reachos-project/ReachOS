# Narrative log — working with the person

## 2026-08-14

**What went wrong.** Asked which of three market definitions to use, offering the three and no
recommendation. The reply took two days and, when it came, was one word.

**The correction.** *"Recommend first, then give me the options."*

**What generalises.** A question with a recommendation attached is answered in a line; a menu is
answered when there is time to think about it. The recommendation is not a presumption — it is
what makes the question cheap to answer. Now written into the shelf's 📋 grammar: *the
question, in one sentence, and the recommendation first.*

## 2026-08-27

**What went wrong.** Delivered a competitor matrix with two cells reading "n/a", and described
them in the covering note as a gap to be closed.

**The correction.** *"That isn't a gap. Those two firms don't publish prices — say that."*

**What generalises.** An absence that has a reason is a **result**, and labelling it a gap
invites someone to spend a week failing to close it. Distinguish, in writing, between *not
found* and *does not exist publicly*. This one has since been promoted out of this log into
the sizing memory, because it turned out to be about the work and not only about us.

## 2026-09-03

**What went wrong.** Asked permission before delegating a market sizing — the fourth time that
week for the same class of request.

**The correction.** *"You have the answer to that already. Stop asking."*

**What generalises.** Not a preference about tone: a standing permission that was recorded at
onboarding and then not honoured, because nothing in the routing map carried it. The fix was
structural, not behavioural — the `Mode` column now says `D` on that row, and the question does
not arise again. ⚠️ A correction that can be fixed in a file should not be fixed in a habit.
